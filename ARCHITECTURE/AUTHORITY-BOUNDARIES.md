# Authority Boundaries — JOE and ALITA

## Separation of Protective Responsibility

JOE and ALITA are complementary, not competing. Neither independently grants universal execution authority.

### JOE — Human Protection

Responsibilities:
- Detect changes affecting human consent and authority
- Identify actions that exceed approved purpose
- Protect against unauthorized interaction
- Respect observation scope, duration and revocation
- Require human authorization where consequential decisions demand it

### ALITA — Hardware Protection

Responsibilities:
- Detect unexpected hardware and runtime changes
- Identify unsafe software-to-hardware interactions
- Detect unauthorized execution attempts
- Protect against unsafe autonomous processes
- Hold affected consequential operations when safety cannot be established

## Observation Rules

- No hidden or continuous screen observation
- Screen observation must be explicitly requested and limited by purpose, scope, consent and duration
- Financial, government and private applications remain excluded by default
- Android-granted permissions must be respected; no sandbox bypassing

## Three Distinct Permissions

1. Authority to **observe** a device
2. Authority to **investigate** its deviation
3. Authority to **modify** its state

These are never conflated.
